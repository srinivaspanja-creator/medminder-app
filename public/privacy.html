Privacy Policy for MyMedMinder

Last updated: June 27, 2026

MyMedMinder ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

MyMedMinder does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or health information on external servers. All data you enter into the App — including medication names, dosages, schedules, appointment details, and prescription photos — is stored locally on your device only, using your browser's local storage.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. We do not have access to your medication records, prescription photos, or any other information you enter into the App.

Prescription Vault Security

The App includes an optional PIN-protected Prescription Vault feature for storing prescription photos. This PIN and any associated security question/answer are also stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Uninstalling the App or clearing your browser/app data will permanently delete all information stored by MyMedMinder. We have no ability to recover or access this data once deleted.

Children's Privacy

MyMedMinder is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Medical Disclaimer

MyMedMinder is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
